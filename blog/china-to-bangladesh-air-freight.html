China to Bangladesh Air Freight: A Complete 2026 Guide for Cross-Border Sellers

Bangladesh is one of the fastest-growing e-commerce markets in South Asia, with garment exports, electronics imports, and a rising middle class driving year-over-year growth. For cross-border sellers, air freight from China to Dhaka is the fastest way to keep shelves stocked — but only if you understand the transit times, cost factors, and customs paperwork involved.

Published 7 September 2026 · Reading time ~7 min · By MJDT Global Logistics Team

Why Ship by Air to Bangladesh?

Air freight is the default choice for e-commerce parcels, samples, mid-value electronics, and any shipment under 500 kg headed for Dhaka, Chittagong, or Sylhet. Bangladesh's seaports are congested, and inland transport from Chittagong to Dhaka can add 5–10 days. By contrast, a direct air shipment from Shenzhen to Dhaka lands in 5–7 days door-to-door.

The trade-off is cost. Air freight typically costs 4–8× more per kg than sea LCL. The right question is not "is air cheaper?" but "does the value of the cargo justify the speed?" For most cross-border e-commerce sellers shipping under 200 kg, the answer is yes — because slow stockouts cost more than the freight premium.

Door-to-door means from your supplier's warehouse in China to your buyer's address in Bangladesh. The breakdown is usually: 1 day pickup, 1–2 days export clearance and airline loading, 1 day flight, 1 day import clearance, 1 day last-mile delivery.

What Can You Ship by Air to Bangladesh?

  • Best fit: Samples, e-commerce parcels 1–100 kg, electronics, mobile accessories, fashion, cosmetics, small machinery parts.
  • Possible but pricier: Furniture, large appliances, anything over 200 kg (consider sea LCL instead).
  • Restricted: Lithium batteries (need IATA DGD declaration), liquids, powders, perishables, branded counterfeit goods.

Cost Factors for 2026

Air freight rates are quoted per kg but billed on the higher of actual weight vs volumetric weight (length × width × height in cm ÷ 6000). A box of 60×40×40 cm = 96,000 ÷ 6000 = 16 volumetric kg, even if it physically weighs 6 kg.

Other cost factors:

  • Fuel surcharge: typically 20–35% of base rate, fluctuates monthly with oil prices.
  • Security surcharge: ~¥1.5/kg for international air cargo security (ICC).
  • Bangladesh destination charges: import clearance, terminal handling, last-mile delivery in Dhaka.
  • Peak season (Oct–Dec): rates can spike 30–50% ahead of Eid and year-end sales.

Bangladesh Customs Clearance — What to Prepare

Bangladesh customs is document-heavy. Missing paperwork is the #1 cause of delays. You will need:

  1. Commercial Invoice — with HS code, unit price, currency, country of origin.
  2. Packing List — gross/net weight, dimensions, carton count.
  3. Bill of Lading / Air Waybill — issued by the carrier.
  4. Import Permit — required for some regulated items (food, medicine, telecom equipment).
  5. Bangladesh Standards (BSTI) — for electronics and food imports, may require pre-shipment testing.

Bangladesh uses the ASYCUDA World system. Duties range from 0% to 25% depending on HS code. Most consumer electronics attract a 5–10% CD + 15% VAT + 5% SD (supplementary duty). A licensed C&F agent in Bangladesh is essential for first-time importers.

Common pitfall: Under-declaring value to save duty. Bangladesh customs routinely opens and re-values shipments, and penalties can be 2–5× the duty owed.
