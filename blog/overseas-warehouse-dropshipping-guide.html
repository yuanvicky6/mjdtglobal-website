Overseas Warehouse Drop Shipping from China: The 2026 Guide for Cross-Border Sellers

Direct drop shipping from China works for the first 100 orders. By the time you hit 1,000, buyers are asking where the package is, and your 30-day delivery time is killing your reviews. Overseas warehouse drop shipping (海外仓代发) is the upgrade that turns cross-border e-commerce from a margin squeeze into a scalable business.

Published 7 September 2026 · Reading time ~8 min · By MJDT Global Logistics Team

What Is Overseas Warehouse Drop Shipping?

Instead of shipping one parcel at a time from a Chinese factory to an overseas customer, you ship in bulk to a warehouse in or near the destination country. The warehouse stores your inventory. When a customer places an order, the warehouse picks, packs, and ships locally — usually arriving in 2–5 days.

The seller (you) never touches the inventory. The warehouse operator handles receiving, storage, labeling, packing, dispatch, and in some cases returns. You pay a per-order fulfillment fee plus storage.

The per-parcel cost of overseas warehouse is comparable to direct shipping — but the customer experience is dramatically better. For Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, and eBay sellers, a 3-day delivery window is now the table stakes, not a luxury.

How the Process Works (Step by Step)

  1. Source your product in China. Use our procurement service if you don't have a factory yet. We can quote, sample, and place orders on your behalf.
  2. Ship bulk to the overseas warehouse. Usually by sea LCL or FCL. Allow 25–35 days for the first bulk shipment to clear customs and land in the warehouse.
  3. Warehouse receives and stocks inventory. The warehouse checks quantities, scans barcodes, photographs any damage, and uploads stock to your WMS / Shopify / Amazon.
  4. Customer places an order on your store. The order is automatically routed (via API or plugin) to the warehouse.
  5. Warehouse picks, packs, labels, and ships. Usually same-day or next-day. Tracking number flows back to your store and to the customer.
  6. Replenish inventory. When stock drops to a threshold, you ship the next bulk from China. Cycle repeats.

What It Costs in 2026

Typical cost breakdown for a 500g parcel from a US-based warehouse:

  • Receiving: $0.50–2.00 per carton (unloaded, counted, scanned).
  • Storage: $0.30–0.80 per cubic foot per month. Most warehouses give 30–90 days free for new SKUs.
  • Pick & pack: $2.00–4.00 per order (single item, polybag or box).
  • Standard last-mile: $3.50–6.00 (USPS Ground, UPS Ground, FedEx Ground) depending on zone and weight.
  • Returns processing: $2.00–5.00 per returned unit (optional service).

Total landed cost for a 500g parcel: roughly $8–15 depending on warehouse and carrier. Compare that to 15–30 day ePacket shipping at $3–8: you pay more, but the conversion rate on a 3-day delivery is typically 2–3× higher.

Who Should Use Overseas Warehouse Drop Shipping

  • Cross-border e-commerce sellers on Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, eBay, Walmart — any platform where fast delivery wins the buy box.
  • Brands launching in a new market who want local fulfillment without opening a local entity.
  • B2B distributors in the destination country who resell to retailers and need a constant stock feed.
  • Subscription box companies that need predictable monthly fulfillment.

Who should not use it: sellers doing under 100 orders per month, sellers of very low-AOV items (under $10) where fulfillment cost erases margin, and sellers of oversized items where the warehousing cost outweighs the speed benefit.

Choosing a Fulfillment Partner

Not all overseas warehouses are equal. What to check:

  1. Integration. Does it plug into your store (Shopify, Amazon, TikTok)? API or plugin? Manual CSV upload is a red flag.
  2. Real-time inventory visibility. You should be able to log in and see stock levels anytime.
  3. Returns policy. Can the warehouse receive returns, inspect, and restock? Or do returns have to come back to China?
  4. Multi-channel capability. Can the same SKU be sold on Shopify and Amazon from one warehouse without commingling?
  5. Customer service. When a buyer emails "where is my order?", who responds?
